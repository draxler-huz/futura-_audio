# Driving Mode Agreement

Driving Mode shows large play, pause, skip, and dismiss controls on the lock screen. Before you turn it on, please read and accept this agreement.

1. I am responsible for using my phone safely at all times, including while driving.
2. I will use Driving Mode only for play, pause, and skip. I will not use the app to browse music, read lyrics, or change settings while the vehicle is moving.
3. I understand the app cannot tell whether I am driving, and does not limit its use based on speed or location.
4. I will keep my attention on the road and will pull over or stop the vehicle before I need to do anything else with the app.
5. I will turn Driving Mode off if it is not safe to use or if local laws restrict using a phone or lock-screen controls while driving.
6. I understand the dismiss button is always available and can be used to close the overlay at any time.
